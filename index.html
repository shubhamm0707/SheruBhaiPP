SHERU BHAI GAME

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Sheru Bhai Game (“the Game”), made by Skorax (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play it on your Android device.

In short: we do not collect, sell or share any personal information. You can play without an account, and your game progress stays on your own phone.

1. Information we collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • There is no sign-in and no account. You can play the whole Game offline after installing it.
  • This version of the Game does not contain advertising or analytics tools, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Information saved only on your phone

To let you continue where you stopped, the Game saves some data inside its own private storage on your device:

  • your game progress and settings (for example sound, controls and weather choices);
  • pictures and posts you make with the in-game “Insta” app. These pictures are taken inside the game world by the Game’s own in-game camera. The Game does not use your phone’s camera, microphone or photo gallery.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your phone’s settings.

3. Permissions

  • Internet and network state – used to show Google Play’s rating window and to open links that you tap.
  • The Game does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Play – the Game is downloaded through Google Play and may show Google Play’s in-app rating window so you can rate the Game. Google handles this under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity – the Game is made with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. We have not turned on Unity’s analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services in this version. Unity’s privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Links you tap – some buttons open other apps or websites, such as Google Play, YouTube, Instagram or Telegram. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

5. Children

The Game is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you think a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • We do not keep any of your data on our servers, because we do not collect it.
  • To delete everything the Game saved on your phone, uninstall the Game or clear its data in your phone’s settings (Settings > Apps > Sheru Bhai Game > Storage > Clear data).
  • If you email us, we use your email address only to reply to you, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

7. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if we add advertising or online features), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change goes live.
